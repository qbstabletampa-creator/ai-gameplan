## Lesson 3: Research Report
1. What source comes first?
   Answer: A. Official source when available

2. What belongs in a source log?
   Answer: A. Title, link, date checked, why it matters

3. What should AI never replace?
   Answer: A. Source verification

2. What is an insight?

3. What should Reagan do with unknowns?
   Answer: A. Label them clearly
