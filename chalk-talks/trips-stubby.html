Trips Stubby

4 Over 3, Split Safety Match

Trips Check|3 to 2 Read|Lock #1

The source calls it Book. We are calling it Stubby. Same idea: take stress off the linebacker versus trips by locking #1 and letting the nickel, Mike, and safety read #3 to #2.

What Is Stubby?

Stubby is a split safety trips match check. The defense builds four over three to the trips side. The corner locks #1, then the inside three defenders distribute the routes by reading #3 to #2.

Why defenses call it

  • It handles trips without giving up easy grass. The defense keeps four over three to the passing strength.
  • It removes #1 from the math. The corner plays MEG on #1, man everywhere he goes.
  • It protects the linebacker. The Mike is not alone on vertical #3. He gets match help from the nickel and safety.

How the QB beats it

  • Do not read it like basic spot drop. The defense is matching, not just sitting in zones.
  • Track #3 first. #3 tells the nickel, Mike, and safety how the coverage will distribute.
  • Find the grass after the match. The best throws show up after the underneath defenders latch or widen.

Coach Cue

Stubby is not Palms to trips. In Palms the corner can trap #2. In Stubby the corner is locked on #1, so the QB has to know who owns #2 after #3 declares.

QB Answers

The QB answer starts with knowing what the coverage is trying to steal.

Corner locked

#1 is not the trap read

The corner is MEG on #1. If you throw #1, you are winning leverage, matchup, or timing. Do not expect the corner to fall off like Palms.

#3 out

Watch the nickel and Mike latch

If #3 and #2 both work out fast, the install wants the nickel and Mike to latch. That can open inside windows if your timing is clean.

#3 vertical

Do not assume Mike alone

Vertical #3 gets matched top down. The safety can squeeze the post or help on the seam. The QB has to confirm depth before forcing it.

Under routes

Find the zone off player

When #3 works under, the defense can pass it and suffocate the catch point. Throw it early, or move to the window behind the match.
