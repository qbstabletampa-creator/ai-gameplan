2x2 Cov 4 Palms

Palms is not just a call. It is a trap door inside the Cover 4 family. Same two high shell. Different answer when #2 releases.

Open the read trainerWatch the clips

What the QB has to know

Do not memorize the defense. Read the moving parts. In 2x2 Palms, the side is built around a 2 to 1 read. The corner and safety are reading #2, then fitting off #1.

Base idea

Palms lives in Cover 4

It starts like quarters, but it can play like quarters or Cover 2 based on #2. That is why the shell can lie to you.

Main key

#2 tells the story

If #2 goes vertical, the safety carries him and the corner stays over #1. If #2 goes to the flat, the corner can squat and the safety overlaps #1.

Do not miss

Fast #3 changes the fit

Fast #3 can trigger push rules. Mike, apex, and safety communication change fast. The QB has to see it before forcing a Cover 4 beater.

Coach cue: Two high does not mean soft. If the corner squats and the safety overlaps, the defense is baiting the outside throw.

QB answers

If #2 vertical

Think quarters

  • Safety is occupied by #2.
  • Corner owns #1 deep.
  • Quick game and matchup answers are clean if leverage allows.
If #2 flat

Expect Cover 2 behavior

  • Corner can squat on the flat.
  • Safety overlaps #1.
  • Be careful with smash unless the corner truly widens and the timing is right.
If #2 radical

Alert the exchange

  • Defense may keep quarters principles.
  • Safety and corner sort routes off #2's stem.
  • Hold the throw until the corner declares.
If #3 is fast

Push rules matter

  • Mike has final #3 or posts #3 based on the call.
  • Apex can carry #2 and alert #3.
  • Do not throw into the push window blind.

Rules from the install

Palms check

If #2 is open, use Palms. If #2 is in the core, the defense can move toward MOD rules.

Stacks

Versus stacks, Palms principles give the defense three over two and keep them over the stack.

Fast #3

If #3 is fast, the defense can push the coverage. That changes who carries, who zones, and who overlaps.

The QB translation

Pre snap, see two high, corner depth, apex location, and #2 alignment. Post snap, read #2 release first. Then confirm corner squat or carry. Then find the void.
