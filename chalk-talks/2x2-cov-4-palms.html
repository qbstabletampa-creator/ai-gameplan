Same family as Cover 4, different trap. Palms lets the corner and safety read #2, then play #1 based on the release.

What Is Palms?

Palms is split field quarters family coverage. To the QB, the lesson is simple. Two high is only the shell. The truth comes from #2's release and the corner's behavior.

Why defenses call it

  • It protects verticals. If #2 goes vertical, the safety can carry him and the corner can stay deep on #1.
  • It traps flat throws. If #2 goes flat, the corner can squat while the safety overlaps #1.
  • It keeps two high disguise. The QB cannot trust shell alone.

How the QB beats it

  • Read #2 first. Vertical, flat, or radical release changes the side.
  • Confirm the corner. Squat means do not throw the outside ball blind.
  • Find the void late. Let the coverage declare, then replace grass.

QB Answers

The answer is not just "throw smash." Palms is built to punish lazy Cover 4 assumptions.

#2 vertical

Work it like quarters

Safety carries #2. Corner has #1. If you have access on the outside, take it. If the safety gets too wide with #2, the inside window can show late.

#2 flat

Respect the trap

Corner squats. Safety overlaps #1. This is where the QB gets baited into throwing the outside route late.

#2 radical

Let the exchange declare

The defense sorts the route. Hold your answer until the corner and safety show who owns #1 and who owns the new inside threat.

Stack

Expect 3 over 2

Palms is a stack check in the install. The defense wants to stay over the stack and avoid getting rubbed. Attack after the traffic clears.

Quick Check

Make sure the QB is reading the coverage, not guessing the shell.

1. What is the first post snap key in Palms?

Correct. #2 tells the corner and safety how the side will play.

2. If #2 releases to the flat, what should the QB expect?

Correct. The corner can squat and the safety overlaps #1.

3. If #2 pushes vertical past 6 yards, what does the side play like?

Correct. Vertical #2 keeps it in the Cover 4 family.

4. What should the QB confirm before throwing an outside Cover 4 answer?

Correct. The corner's behavior tells you whether the outside throw is clean or baited.

5. Versus stack or tight split, what is Palms trying to create?

Correct. The install keeps three over two so the defense can handle traffic.
