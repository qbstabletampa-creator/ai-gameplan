## Lesson 1: Sports Brand Audit
**Time:** 60 minutes

**Goal:** Reagan audits a college athletics social account and learns what strong sports content does.

### Work
#### Pick the account
Choose one college athletics department or team account. Use public information only.

#### Audit the basics
Review bio, pinned posts, last 10 posts, video usage, graphics, calls to action, and fan engagement.

#### Use AI the right way
Ask Claude to organize your notes, not invent facts. Paste only public observations.

#### Submit
Turn in a one page audit with 3 strengths, 3 misses, and 3 fixes.

## Lesson 5: Simple Automation Map
**Time:** 90 minutes

**Goal:** Reagan designs one safe automation map for a content workflow.

### Work
#### Pick the repeatable job
Choose one job: collect links, draft captions, save screenshots, or build a weekly report.

#### Map inputs and outputs
Write what starts the automation, what tools it touches, and what the final output should be.

#### Add safety rules
No public posting, no private data, and human review before anything goes out.
