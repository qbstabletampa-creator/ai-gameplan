# Week 1 Rubric

CJ grades like a coach, not a professor.

Score each area 1 to 4.

## 4, Exceeded

Better than expected. Clear, useful, and could be shown to someone outside the course.

## 3, Met

Complete and solid. Shows real understanding.

## 2, Needs work

Started, but missing clarity, quality, or follow through.

## 1, Incomplete

Not turned in or not enough to review.
