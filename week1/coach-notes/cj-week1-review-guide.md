# CJ Coach Notes: Week 1 Review

## What this week is really testing

Not coding talent.

You are checking if Reagan can:

- learn a new tool
- follow a process
- save work
- explain her thinking
- ask for help without freezing
- turn AI output into something useful

## Friday review flow

1. Ask her to explain LLM vs agent in 60 seconds.
2. Have her open her GitHub repo.
3. Have her show her README.
4. Have her show one Claude Code task.
5. Have her show one Codex task.
6. Ask which tool she trusted more and why.
7. Have her explain Hermes in plain English.
8. Have her demo her project choice.
9. Grade the rubric.
10. Give one thing to clean up before Week 2.

## Good signs

- She can explain without reading word for word.
- She knows where her files are.
- Her README sounds like her.
- She can say what the AI got wrong.
- She asks better questions by Friday than she did Monday.

## Red flags

- She only watched videos and did not build.
- She copied AI output without understanding it.
- She cannot find her own files.
- She has no GitHub repo.
- She got stuck and stayed stuck.

## CJ line to use

Rea, I do not need perfect. I need proof you can learn, build, save your work, and explain what you did.
