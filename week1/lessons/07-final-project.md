# Lesson 1.7: Week 1 Final Project Sprint

## Goal

Reagan builds a small but real project that connects AI to college athletics.

She chooses one path.

## Option A: Sports Ops Assistant

Build a simple assistant plan that helps a college athletics staff run better.

It can help with:

- game day checklist
- travel checklist
- practice schedule outline
- equipment checklist
- staff task list
- social content reminders

Deliverable:

```text
projects/sports-ops-assistant.md
```

Must include:

- what problem it solves
- who uses it
- 5 tasks it can help with
- sample prompt library
- safety rules
- future version ideas

## Option B: Job Researcher

Build a simple assistant plan that helps Reagan find college athletics jobs.

It can help with:

- target schools
- department research
- job descriptions
- outreach notes
- application tracker
- interview prep

Deliverable:

```text
projects/job-researcher.md
```

Must include:

- what problem it solves
- what jobs it looks for
- 10 target organizations
- research fields to collect
- outreach message template
- weekly job search workflow

## Friday demo

Reagan presents for 5 to 7 minutes:

1. What she built.
2. Why she chose it.
3. What tool helped most.
4. What was hard.
5. What she would build next.

## Done looks like

This is good enough to show someone in athletics and say, "I built this in Week 1."
